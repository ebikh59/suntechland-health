A recent webinar highlights the importance of HIPAA-compliant email for small healthcare practices in the US, but Australian healthcare providers should also be aware of the need to protect sensitive patient information. While the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is US legislation, the principles of securing patient data are equally relevant in Australia under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Many small healthcare practices may assume their email systems are secure, but gaps in security can put patient privacy at risk and leave practices vulnerable to regulatory penalties. The Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme in Australia requires healthcare providers to notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals in the event of a data breach. Implementing secure email systems can be challenging, particularly for small practices without in-house IT staff. However, there are steps that can be taken to close security gaps and ensure compliance with Australian privacy regulations. The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) provides guidance on securing email systems, including the use of the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies. By following these strategies and seeking expert advice, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and protect sensitive patient information. The Secure Online Communication Initiative (SOCI) also provides a framework for secure online communication in healthcare. As the healthcare sector continues to evolve and become increasingly reliant on digital technologies, the need for robust cybersecurity measures has never been more critical. Australian healthcare providers must prioritise the security of patient data and take proactive steps to prevent data breaches. By doing so, they can maintain the trust of their patients and ensure the long-term sustainability of their practices. The importance of securing email systems cannot be overstated, and Australian healthcare providers must take immediate action to address any security gaps and ensure compliance with relevant regulations. This includes implementing secure email solutions, providing staff training on email security, and regularly reviewing and updating cybersecurity protocols. By taking these steps, Australian healthcare providers can protect patient data and maintain the integrity of their practices.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to protect against cyber threats, including the use of application whitelisting and patching of operating systems and applications
- Regularly review and update email security protocols to ensure compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
- Provide staff training on email security best practices, including how to identify and report suspicious emails, to reduce the risk of data breaches and maintain the security of patient information
