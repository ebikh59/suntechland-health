A historic phishing attack on Heart Care Centers of Illinois has exposed the personal and protected health information of certain patients, highlighting the importance of robust cybersecurity measures in the healthcare sector. The attack, which occurred between August 22, 2024, and November 6, 2024, was only discovered on January 15, 2026, after an investigation into an unsuccessful phishing attempt. The breach has significant implications for Australian healthcare providers, who must ensure compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, as well as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. The types of information exposed in the breach, including names, addresses, and health insurance information, are also protected under Australian law, and healthcare providers must take steps to prevent similar breaches. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for healthcare providers to protect themselves against cyber threats, and following this guidance is crucial in preventing similar breaches. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act highlights the need for healthcare providers to have robust cybersecurity measures in place to protect sensitive information. Australian healthcare providers must review their existing policies and procedures to reduce the risk of similar incidents in the future, and consider implementing additional security measures such as multi-factor authentication and regular security audits. By taking proactive steps to protect against cyber threats, healthcare providers can minimize the risk of a breach and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive patient information. The incident serves as a reminder of the importance of ongoing cybersecurity monitoring and the need for healthcare providers to be vigilant in detecting and responding to potential security incidents. In the event of a breach, healthcare providers must be prepared to notify affected individuals and provide support, such as complimentary credit monitoring and identity restoration services, as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information
- Conduct regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities
- Provide ongoing cybersecurity awareness training to employees to prevent phishing attacks
