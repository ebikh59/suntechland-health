A recent settlement in the United States has highlighted the importance of robust cybersecurity measures in healthcare, with OSF Healthcare System agreeing to pay $552,250 to resolve alleged violations of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules. The settlement stems from a ransomware attack in April 2021, which resulted in the exfiltration of protected health information (PHI) of 53,907 patients, including sensitive details such as names, driver's license numbers, and health insurance information. The Office for Civil Rights (OCR) investigation found that OSF Healthcare had failed to conduct a comprehensive risk analysis, leading to an impermissible disclosure of PHI. This case serves as a reminder to Australian healthcare providers of the need to prioritise cybersecurity and comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, particularly in relation to the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme. The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) and the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) provide guidance on essential cybersecurity measures, including the ASD Essential Eight, which can help healthcare organisations mitigate the risk of cyber attacks. By implementing these measures, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient information. The settlement also underscores the importance of timely notification in the event of a data breach, as required by the NDB scheme and the Security of Critical Infrastructure (SOCI) Act. Australian healthcare organisations must be prepared to respond quickly and effectively in the event of a cyber attack, to minimise harm to patients and maintain trust in the healthcare system.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Conduct regular and comprehensive risk analyses to identify vulnerabilities and mitigate security risks
- Implement the ASD Essential Eight cybersecurity measures to prevent and detect cyber threats
- Develop and test incident response plans to ensure timely notification and effective response in the event of a data breach
