Australian healthcare providers should note that while the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a US law, its principles can inform local compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The US Department of Health and Human Services' Seven Fundamental Elements of an Effective Compliance Program offers a useful framework for achieving compliance. This framework includes developing policies and procedures, designating privacy and security officers, implementing effective training programs, and ensuring channels of communication exist for reporting violations and breaches. For Australian healthcare organisations, this translates into ensuring they have robust policies in place for handling personal health information, appointing privacy and security officers, and providing ongoing training to staff. The Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme also requires organisations to have procedures in place for responding to data breaches promptly. By adopting a similar framework, Australian healthcare providers can better protect patient data and maintain compliance with local regulations. The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) also provides guidance on protecting against cyber threats, including the importance of implementing the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies. Australian healthcare providers should consider these strategies in conjunction with the principles outlined in the US framework to ensure comprehensive protection of patient data. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure (SOCI) Act 2018 imposes additional obligations on certain healthcare providers, making it essential to integrate these compliance requirements into their overall risk management strategy. By taking a proactive and multi-faceted approach to compliance and cybersecurity, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and maintain trust with their patients.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to protect against cyber threats
- Develop and regularly review policies and procedures for handling personal health information, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles
- Provide ongoing training to staff on cybersecurity best practices and the importance of protecting patient data, as outlined in ACSC guidance
