A recent study by Check Point has revealed that ChatGPT has entered the top 10 most impersonated brands in phishing attacks for the first time in the second quarter of 2026. This development is significant for Australian healthcare providers, as it highlights the growing threat of phishing attacks targeting popular brands, including those used in the healthcare sector. The study found that scammers are impersonating trusted brands, such as Microsoft and Google, to steal login credentials, payment details, or personal information. Australian healthcare providers must be vigilant in protecting their patients' personal information, as required by the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also mandates that healthcare providers notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. To mitigate the threat of brand phishing, Australian healthcare providers can implement robust cybersecurity measures, such as stopping phishing messages inline and using AI-powered detection to catch brand impersonation. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for healthcare providers to protect their systems and data from cyber threats. Furthermore, consolidating email and workspace protection across different platforms can reduce operational complexity and improve incident response. As AI tools become more prevalent in healthcare, it is essential for providers to stay ahead of emerging threats and prioritise cybersecurity to safeguard sensitive patient information. By following the ACSC guidance and implementing the recommended cybersecurity measures, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of brand phishing attacks and protect their patients' personal information. The Security of Critical Infrastructure Act also emphasizes the importance of cybersecurity in critical infrastructure sectors, including healthcare. Australian healthcare providers must take proactive steps to prevent brand phishing attacks and ensure the security and integrity of their systems and data.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement AI-powered detection to catch brand impersonation and phishing attacks
- Consolidate email and workspace protection across different platforms to reduce operational complexity
- Automate investigation and response to resolve real threats faster and improve incident response
