A recent ransomware attack on the Texas Hearing Institute has compromised the protected health information of nearly 30,000 patients, highlighting the ongoing threat of cyberattacks to healthcare providers. The incident, which was identified on March 20, 2026, involved the unauthorized access of certain parts of the institute's network, including files containing patient information such as names, personal identifiers, Social Security numbers, diagnosis and treatment information, and financial account information. The affected individuals have been offered complimentary credit monitoring services to mitigate the risk of identity theft and fraud. This incident serves as a reminder to Australian healthcare providers of the importance of robust cybersecurity measures, including the implementation of the ASD Essential Eight, to protect sensitive patient information. Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, Australian healthcare providers are required to notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals in the event of a data breach. The Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles also impose obligations on healthcare providers to ensure the secure handling of personal information. The Texas Hearing Institute incident also underscores the need for healthcare providers to have incident response plans in place to respond quickly and effectively in the event of a cyberattack. Australian healthcare providers should review their cybersecurity protocols and ensure they are aligned with ACSC guidance and the SOCI Act to prevent similar incidents. By prioritising cybersecurity, Australian healthcare providers can protect patient information and maintain trust in the healthcare system. The incident also highlights the importance of being aware of the latest threats, including ransomware-as-a-service groups like Interlock, which claimed responsibility for the attack on the Texas Hearing Institute. Australian healthcare providers should stay informed about emerging threats and take proactive steps to prevent and respond to cyberattacks.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight cybersecurity mitigation strategies to prevent ransomware attacks
- Develop and regularly test incident response plans to ensure effective response to cyberattacks
- Provide regular cybersecurity awareness training to staff to prevent phishing and other social engineering attacks
