A recent settlement has been reached in class action data breach lawsuits against Omni Healthcare Financial Holdings and its subsidiaries, as well as Western Montana Clinic. The lawsuits stem from a cybersecurity incident in January 2024, which exposed the protected health information of 16,852 individuals. The incident involved unauthorized network access and resulted in the exposure of sensitive information, including names, contact details, dates of birth, and medical record numbers. Australian healthcare providers should take note of this incident, as it highlights the importance of implementing robust cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive patient information. Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, healthcare providers have a responsibility to notify affected individuals in the event of a data breach. The settlement reached in this case includes provisions for affected individuals to receive medical data monitoring and medical identity theft services, as well as cash payments for documented losses. This serves as a reminder to Australian healthcare providers of the potential consequences of a data breach and the need to prioritise cybersecurity. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for healthcare providers to follow in order to mitigate the risk of a cybersecurity incident. By following these guidelines and staying up to date with the latest ACSC guidance, healthcare providers can reduce the risk of a data breach and ensure compliance with relevant legislation, including the SOCI Act. The settlement also highlights the importance of prompt notification of affected individuals, with the incident in question resulting in notifications being mailed 15 months after the breach was first detected. Australian healthcare providers should be aware of their obligations under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and ensure that they have procedures in place to respond quickly and effectively in the event of a data breach. The incident and subsequent settlement serve as a reminder of the importance of prioritising cybersecurity and patient data protection in the healthcare sector. By taking proactive steps to protect patient information, healthcare providers can reduce the risk of a data breach and ensure compliance with relevant legislation. The Australian healthcare sector should take note of this incident and review their own cybersecurity measures to ensure they are adequately protecting sensitive patient information.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to reduce the risk of a cybersecurity incident
- Review and update incident response plans to ensure compliance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and prompt notification of affected individuals
- Conduct regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and prioritise patient data protection
