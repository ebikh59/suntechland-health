A recent data breach settlement in the United States has significant implications for Australian healthcare providers. ZOLL Medical Corporation, a global medical device and software company, has agreed to pay $3.5 million to settle a class action lawsuit related to a data breach that exposed the personally identifiable information and protected health information of over 1 million individuals. The breach, which occurred in January 2023, was caused by unauthorized network access and resulted in the exposure of sensitive information, including names, addresses, dates of birth, and Social Security numbers. This incident highlights the importance of robust cybersecurity measures in protecting sensitive healthcare information, as required by the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988. Australian healthcare providers must ensure they have adequate safeguards in place to prevent similar breaches, which could lead to significant financial and reputational consequences, as well as obligations under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. The settlement also underscores the need for timely and transparent breach notification, as mandated by the NDB scheme, to allow affected individuals to take steps to protect themselves against potential misuse of their information. Furthermore, this incident demonstrates the importance of implementing the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies and following ACSC guidance to prevent and respond to cybersecurity incidents. Australian healthcare providers should review their cybersecurity protocols and ensure they are aligned with these guidelines to minimize the risk of a similar breach occurring. The Security of Critical Infrastructure Act also plays a critical role in ensuring the security and resilience of healthcare infrastructure, and providers must be aware of their obligations under this legislation. By prioritizing cybersecurity and complying with relevant regulations, Australian healthcare providers can protect sensitive patient information and maintain trust in the healthcare system.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies, including patching applications and operating systems, to prevent cybersecurity incidents
- Regularly review and update incident response plans to ensure compliance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and ACSC guidance
- Conduct a risk assessment to identify vulnerabilities in healthcare infrastructure and address them in accordance with the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act
