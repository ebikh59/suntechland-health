A recent webinar has highlighted the importance of being prepared for investigations into data breaches in the healthcare sector, particularly in light of the rise of ransomware attacks and patient complaints. While the webinar specifically focuses on HIPAA investigations in the United States, the lessons learned can also be applied to Australian healthcare providers, who must comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme also requires healthcare providers to notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals in the event of a data breach. The webinar, which is based on real experience from over 250 actual OCR investigations, provides insights into what happens during an investigation and where organisations often fail, allowing healthcare providers to avoid government fines and drawn-out investigations. Australian healthcare providers can learn from the webinar by understanding the importance of having a robust compliance posture and being prepared to respond to investigations. This includes having incident response plans in place, regularly reviewing and updating policies and procedures, and providing training to staff on data handling and protection. By taking these steps, healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and ensure they are well-equipped to respond in the event of an investigation. The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) also provides guidance on how to protect against cyber threats, including the implementation of the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies. By following these strategies and staying informed about the latest threats and vulnerabilities, healthcare providers can reduce the risk of a data breach and ensure they are well-prepared to respond to an investigation. The Security of Critical Infrastructure (SOCI) Act also requires healthcare providers to take steps to protect their systems and data from cyber threats, and to report any incidents to the relevant authorities.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to reduce the risk of cyber threats
- Regularly review and update incident response plans to ensure preparedness in the event of a data breach
- Provide training to staff on data handling and protection to reduce the risk of human error
