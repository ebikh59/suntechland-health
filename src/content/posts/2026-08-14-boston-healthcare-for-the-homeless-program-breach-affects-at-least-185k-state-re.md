A significant data breach has been reported by the Boston Healthcare for the Homeless Program, affecting at least 185,000 state residents in Massachusetts. The breach, which was first identified on November 11, 2025, involved an unauthorised third party accessing the organisation's network and potentially viewing or obtaining sensitive patient information, including names, social security numbers, and health records. This incident highlights the importance of robust cybersecurity measures for Australian healthcare providers, particularly in protecting sensitive patient information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires organisations to notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach, similar to the notifications issued by the Boston Healthcare for the Homeless Program. Australian healthcare providers should review their cybersecurity protocols to prevent similar incidents, considering guidance from the Australian Cyber Security Centre and the implementation of the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 also plays a crucial role in ensuring the security and resilience of healthcare infrastructure. The breach at Boston Healthcare for the Homeless Program serves as a reminder for Australian healthcare organisations to be vigilant and proactive in their cybersecurity efforts, including regular monitoring for suspicious activity and providing staff training to prevent phishing attacks. By prioritising cybersecurity, Australian healthcare providers can better protect patient information and maintain trust in the healthcare system. The incident also underscores the need for comprehensive incident response plans, including procedures for notifying affected individuals and providing support, such as credit monitoring services, in the event of a breach. Australian healthcare providers must remain aware of the evolving cybersecurity landscape and take proactive steps to enhance their security posture, including implementing robust access controls, regularly updating software, and conducting thorough risk assessments.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive patient information
- Conduct regular staff training on cybersecurity best practices, including identifying and responding to phishing attacks
- Review and update incident response plans to ensure compliance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and the Australian Privacy Principles
