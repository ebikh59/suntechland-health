A recent development in the United States has sparked concerns over patient data privacy, with the US Consumer Product Safety Commission requesting digital patient data from hospitals to track consumer product-related injuries. Although this initiative is not directly related to Australian healthcare, it highlights the importance of protecting sensitive patient information. In Australia, healthcare providers must comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, which dictate how personal information should be handled. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also requires organisations to notify individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. As Australian healthcare providers increasingly rely on digital systems, they must ensure that their data collection and storage practices are secure and compliant with relevant regulations. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight guidelines provide a framework for organisations to protect themselves against cyber threats, and healthcare providers should prioritise implementing these measures to safeguard patient data. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act highlights the need for healthcare providers to be vigilant about their data security, given the critical nature of their services. By prioritising data protection and cybersecurity, Australian healthcare providers can maintain the trust of their patients and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive information.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight guidelines to protect against cyber threats, including patching applications, restricting administrative privileges, and configuring Microsoft Office macro settings
- Regularly review and update data collection and storage practices to ensure compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles
- Conduct risk assessments and implement additional security measures to protect sensitive patient data, as recommended by the Australian Cyber Security Centre
