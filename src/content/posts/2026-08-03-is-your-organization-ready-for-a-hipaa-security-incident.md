A key aspect of healthcare cybersecurity is being prepared for security incidents, which is crucial for Australian healthcare providers in protecting sensitive patient information. Although the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is US legislation, its principles can inform Australian healthcare providers about the importance of risk assessments in ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic personal health information (ePHI). In Australia, the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) govern the handling of personal information, including health information. The Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme also requires entities to notify individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) in the event of a data breach. Conducting regular security risk assessments, akin to HIPAA security risk assessments, can help Australian healthcare providers identify vulnerabilities and Vreduce risks to a reasonable and appropriate level, thus complying with the APPs and the NDB scheme. The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) provides guidance on cybersecurity, including the Essential Eight mitigation strategies, which can help healthcare providers protect against cyber threats. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure (SOCI) Act 2018 is another piece of legislation that Australian healthcare providers should be aware of, as it aims to improve the security and resilience of critical infrastructure, including healthcare. By understanding and applying these principles and guidelines, Australian healthcare providers can better protect patient information and maintain trust in the healthcare system. It is essential for healthcare providers to regularly review and update their cybersecurity practices to ensure they are aligned with the latest threats and regulations. This includes providing security awareness training to their workforce and enforcing a sanctions policy to ensure compliance with relevant legislation and guidelines. Ultimately, being prepared for security incidents is an ongoing process that requires continuous monitoring, assessment, and improvement of cybersecurity practices.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Conduct regular security risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and reduce risks to a reasonable and appropriate level
- Implement the ACSC's Essential Eight mitigation strategies to protect against cyber threats
- Provide security awareness training to workforce and enforce a sanctions policy to ensure compliance with relevant legislation and guidelines
