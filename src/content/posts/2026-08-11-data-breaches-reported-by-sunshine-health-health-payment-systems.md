A recent vishing attack on a US-based health insurance agency has highlighted the importance of robust cybersecurity measures for Australian healthcare providers. The attack on Sunshine Health, a Florida-based Medicaid and health insurance agency, resulted in the theft of protected health information of over 41,000 individuals. Similarly, Health Payment Systems, a Wisconsin healthcare billing company, experienced an email security incident affecting over 8,000 patients. These incidents demonstrate the need for Australian healthcare organisations to be vigilant in protecting sensitive patient information, as required by the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also mandates that entities notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. Australian healthcare providers can draw lessons from these incidents, particularly in regards to the importance of employee training and awareness of phishing attacks, as well as the need for robust email security measures. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for organisations to protect themselves against cyber threats, and healthcare providers should ensure they are implementing these measures to prevent similar incidents. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act highlights the need for healthcare organisations to prioritise cybersecurity as part of their overall risk management strategy. By taking proactive steps to protect patient information, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and maintain trust with their patients.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement regular employee training and awareness programs to prevent phishing and vishing attacks, as recommended by the ACSC
- Review and strengthen email security measures, including multi-factor authentication and email encryption, to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive patient information
- Conduct regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and ensure compliance with the ASD Essential Eight and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
