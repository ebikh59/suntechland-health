A significant data breach has occurred at Brown Health Medical Group-MA, a US-based medical group, potentially compromising the protected health information of almost 312,000 individuals. The breach, which was detected in December 2025, involved unauthorized access to a legacy file server and resulted in the exposure of sensitive information, including names, dates of birth, contact information, Social Security numbers, and financial account information. Although the electronic medical record system was not involved, the breach highlights the importance of robust cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive health information. For Australian healthcare providers, this incident serves as a reminder of the need to comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, as well as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. The incident also underscores the importance of implementing the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to prevent similar breaches. Australian healthcare providers should review their own cybersecurity protocols to ensure they are taking adequate steps to protect sensitive health information. The provision of complimentary credit monitoring and identity theft protection services to affected individuals is a useful reminder of the need for Australian healthcare providers to have a robust response plan in place in the event of a data breach. The Security of Critical Infrastructure Act (SOCI Act) also requires certain healthcare providers to implement robust cybersecurity measures, and this incident highlights the importance of compliance with this legislation.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies, including patching applications and operating systems, to prevent similar breaches
- Review and update incident response plans to ensure they are adequate and compliant with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
- Conduct regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and ensure compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles
