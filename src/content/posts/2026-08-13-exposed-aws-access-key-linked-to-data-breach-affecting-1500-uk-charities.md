A recent cyber-attack on UK-based CRM provider Beacon has exposed the personal information of over 1500 charities, including those operating in sensitive areas such as healthcare and victim support. The breach is believed to have been caused by a compromised AWS access key, which was potentially exposed in public Javascript build artifacts due to an error during software development. As a result, the attacker was able to access and download all data contained within the CRM platform, including attachment files, using valid credentials. Although the data was encrypted at rest in AWS, the threat actor's valid credentials allowed for decryption, making the data readable. Australian healthcare providers should be aware of the potential risks associated with cloud storage and take steps to ensure the security of their own data, particularly in light of the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also requires organisations to notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. The ASD Essential Eight and ACSC guidance provide valuable resources for organisations to improve their cybersecurity posture and prevent similar breaches. In this case, the breach highlights the importance of secure credential management and the need for regular monitoring of cloud storage activity. Australian healthcare providers should review their own cloud storage practices and ensure that they are taking adequate measures to protect sensitive patient information. The SOCI Act also plays a critical role in ensuring the security of sensitive information, and organisations must be aware of their obligations under this legislation. By prioritising cybersecurity and taking proactive steps to protect sensitive data, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of a similar breach occurring in their own organisations.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorised access to cloud storage
- Regularly review and monitor cloud storage activity to detect potential security incidents
- Ensure secure credential management practices are in place, including regular rotation of access keys and passwords
