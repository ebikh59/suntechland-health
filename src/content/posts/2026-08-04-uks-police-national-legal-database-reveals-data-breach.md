A significant data breach has occurred in the UK's Police National Legal Database, compromising the names, organisations, and work email addresses of police officers, staff, and other criminal justice professionals. The breach, which was identified on July 26, has resulted in the publication of the stolen data on the dark web. The incident has implications for Australian healthcare providers, who must be vigilant in protecting sensitive information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires healthcare providers to notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. The ASD Essential Eight and ACSC guidance provide a framework for healthcare providers to mitigate cyber threats, including implementing multi-factor authentication and regularly updating software. In the context of this breach, Australian healthcare providers should be aware of the potential for targeted spear-phishing and social engineering attacks, and take steps to educate staff on how to identify and respond to these threats. The SOCI Act also plays a crucial role in the protection of sensitive information, and healthcare providers must ensure they are complying with its requirements. As the UK government considers introducing a ban on public sector organisations paying extortion demands, Australian healthcare providers should also consider their own approach to managing cyber threats and responding to data breaches. By prioritising cybersecurity and implementing robust protections, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of a data breach and protect sensitive information.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive information
- Regularly update software and systems to ensure they are protected against known vulnerabilities
- Educate staff on how to identify and respond to targeted spear-phishing and social engineering attacks
