A significant development in the protection of health information has occurred in the US, with the Health Information Privacy Reform Act being advanced by a Senate committee. This proposed legislation aims to improve privacy protections for health data not currently covered by existing regulations, such as that collected by health apps and wearable devices. The Act seeks to address the current gaps in privacy protections by expanding the coverage of health data to include 'consumer health data' collected by non-regulated entities. For Australian healthcare providers, this development highlights the importance of ensuring the privacy and security of health information, as required by the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also mandates that healthcare providers notify individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in the event of a data breach. As the use of health apps and wearable devices becomes more prevalent in Australia, healthcare providers must be aware of the potential risks to health information and take steps to mitigate them. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for protecting against cyber threats, and healthcare providers should consider implementing these measures to safeguard health information. The Security of Critical Infrastructure Act also imposes obligations on healthcare providers to protect their systems and data from cyber threats.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to protect against cyber threats, including patching applications and operating systems, and restricting administrative privileges
- Conduct regular risk assessments to identify potential vulnerabilities in health information systems and implement measures to mitigate them
- Develop and implement policies and procedures for the secure collection, storage, and disclosure of health information, in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
