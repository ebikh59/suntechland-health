Australian digital health company WeGuide has secured $1.95 million in CUREator+ funding to develop a platform designed to speed up the development and commercialisation of multi-modal digital biomarkers. The platform will use AI-powered agentic workflows to automate parts of the biomarker development process, including data cleaning and the generation of algorithmic variants from multi-modal datasets.
