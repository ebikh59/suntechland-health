A significant cyber incident has impacted Beacon, a third-party CRM provider used by around 1500 UK charities, including those in the healthcare and victim support sectors. The incident has resulted in the potential data breaches of these charities, with personal details such as names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and donation records believed to have been accessed, copied, and likely exfiltrated by an unauthorized actor. Australian healthcare providers should be aware of the potential risks associated with using third-party providers and the importance of ensuring the security of sensitive information. The incident highlights the need for robust cybersecurity measures, including the implementation of the ASD Essential Eight, to protect against cyber threats. Under the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, Australian healthcare providers have a responsibility to protect sensitive information and notify individuals in the event of a data breach, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. The compromised CRM system in this incident did not hold sensitive patient information, but it is a reminder for Australian healthcare providers to review their own systems and ensure they are secure. The ACSC guidance and the SOCI Act also provide frameworks for managing and responding to cybersecurity incidents. Australian healthcare providers should take note of the incident and review their own cybersecurity measures to ensure they are adequate. The use of encrypted data and secure payment systems is crucial in preventing such incidents. The incident has been contained, and an investigation is underway, but it serves as a reminder for Australian healthcare providers to be vigilant and proactive in protecting sensitive information. By following the ASD Essential Eight and ACSC guidance, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of cyber incidents and protect sensitive information.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Review and assess the security of all third-party providers used by the organisation
- Implement robust access controls, including multi-factor authentication, to prevent unauthorized access to systems and data
- Regularly update and patch systems, and ensure that all software and applications are up-to-date and secure, in line with the ASD Essential Eight
