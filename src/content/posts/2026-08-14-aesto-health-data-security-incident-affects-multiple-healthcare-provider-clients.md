A significant data security incident has occurred at Aesto Health, a healthcare technology company, affecting numerous Australian healthcare providers' clients who utilise their services. The breach, which was identified on or around December 18, 2025, involved unauthorised access to Aesto Health's Amazon Web Services infrastructure, resulting in the exposure of personally identifiable information and protected health information. This incident highlights the importance of robust cybersecurity measures for healthcare providers in Australia, particularly in light of the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988. Australian healthcare providers must ensure they are complying with these regulations and taking proactive steps to protect sensitive patient data. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight guidelines provide a framework for organisations to mitigate cyber threats, and healthcare providers should prioritise implementing these measures to prevent similar breaches. Furthermore, the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 also plays a crucial role in ensuring the security of healthcare infrastructure, and providers must be aware of their obligations under this legislation. As the Australian healthcare sector continues to rely on digital systems, the risk of cyber attacks and data breaches will only continue to grow, making it essential for providers to stay vigilant and proactive in their cybersecurity efforts. The fact that Aesto Health's breach has affected hundreds of thousands of patients worldwide serves as a stark reminder of the potential consequences of a data breach, and Australian healthcare providers must take heed of this incident to review and strengthen their own cybersecurity practices. In order to mitigate the risk of similar incidents, Australian healthcare providers should focus on enhancing their cybersecurity posture, including regularly reviewing and updating their security protocols, conducting thorough risk assessments, and providing ongoing training to staff on cybersecurity best practices.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorised access to sensitive data and systems, as recommended by the ASD Essential Eight
- Regularly conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify and address potential security weaknesses, in line with ACSC guidance
- Develop and maintain a comprehensive incident response plan to ensure effective response and notification in the event of a data breach, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
