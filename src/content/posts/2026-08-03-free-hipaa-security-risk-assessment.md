A free HIPAA security risk assessment is being offered, highlighting the importance of assessing threats to the privacy and security of protected health information. While the HIPAA Security Rule and Breach Notification Rule require covered entities and business associates to conduct risk assessments, Australian healthcare providers should also consider the risks to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal health information. Under the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, healthcare providers have a responsibility to protect sensitive information, and conducting regular risk assessments is essential to identifying vulnerabilities. The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme also requires healthcare providers to notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals in the event of a data breach. Australian healthcare providers can learn from the HIPAA security risk assessment framework, which aims to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information. By adopting a similar approach, healthcare providers can protect against reasonably anticipated threats and ensure compliance with the Australian privacy regulatory framework. The Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight provides a framework for healthcare providers to protect their systems and data, and conducting regular risk assessments is a key part of this framework. By prioritising cybersecurity and conducting regular risk assessments, Australian healthcare providers can reduce the risk of data breaches and protect sensitive patient information.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Conduct regular security risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and threats to sensitive patient information
- Implement the ASD Essential Eight mitigation strategies to protect against cyber threats
- Provide security awareness training to workforce members to ensure compliance with the Australian privacy regulatory framework
