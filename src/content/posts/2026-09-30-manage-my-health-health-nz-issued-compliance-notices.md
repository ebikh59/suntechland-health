New Zealand Privacy Commissioner Michael Webster has issued compliance notices to Health NZ and Manage My Health over security failings identified following the December 2025 Manage My Health cyber attack. The notices relate to failures to comply with security requirements under rule 5 of the Health Information Privacy Code at the time of the attack. […]
