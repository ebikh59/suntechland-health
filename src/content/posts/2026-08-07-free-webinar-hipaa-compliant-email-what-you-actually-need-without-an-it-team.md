A recent webinar highlighted the importance of HIPAA-compliant email for small healthcare practices in the United States, a concern also relevant to Australian healthcare providers given the need to protect sensitive patient information. The webinar, now available on demand, aimed to educate practice owners, office managers, and administrators on how to make their emails compliant with HIPAA without requiring an in-house IT team. Although the discussion was centred around HIPAA, the principles of ensuring the privacy and security of patient information are equally applicable in Australia under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Australian healthcare providers must also comply with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, which mandates the reporting of certain data breaches to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. The webinar's focus on easily closing security gaps and implementing secure email solutions resonates with the Australian Cyber Security Centre's (ACSC) guidance on protecting against cyber threats. Moreover, the importance of secure email systems aligns with the ASD Essential Eight, a series of mitigation strategies designed to help organisations protect themselves against cyber threats. For Australian healthcare providers, ensuring the security of patient information is not only a legal requirement but also essential for maintaining trust and integrity in the healthcare system. By adopting robust cybersecurity measures, including secure email solutions, healthcare providers can better protect patient data and comply with relevant regulations, including the Security of Critical Infrastructure (SOCI) Act. This involves understanding what constitutes a secure and compliant email system, the limitations of standard email services like Gmail and Microsoft 365 in terms of compliance, and how to select an appropriate email solution without needing dedicated IT staff. Ultimately, the protection of patient information requires a proactive and informed approach to cybersecurity, one that Australian healthcare providers must prioritize to meet their obligations under Australian law and to safeguard the trust of their patients.

## What Australian Healthcare Organisations Should Do

- Implement multi-factor authentication to protect access to email systems, as recommended by the ASD Essential Eight
- Regularly review and update email security policies to ensure compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
- Assess current email solutions against the ACSC's guidance on secure email practices to identify and address potential security gaps
